📊 Level 2 Market Depth: How to Read & Trade

A visual guide to interpreting order book data and executing scalping strategies.

This chart is a map of the battlefield (showing current buyer/seller pressure), not the war itself. Use these strategies to interpret the data.

Part 1: How to Read the Data (The Map)

The Columns (Left to Right)

🟢 Bids (Green Bars)

Buyers. The numbers show the exact quantity of contracts traders are willing to buy at that specific price. The length of the bar shows the relative size of that buyer.

🔴 Asks (Red Bars)

Sellers. The numbers show the quantity of contracts traders are willing to sell at that specific price. The length of the bar shows the relative size of that seller.

📈 Imbalance, % (The Most Important Column)

This calculates the difference between Bids and Asks at that price level.

  • High (Green): More buyers than sellers. Acts as a support floor.
  • High (Red): More sellers than buyers. Acts as a resistance ceiling.

The Most Important Row (The Yellow Line)

🟡 Current Market Price

Example: Price 7,554.25. Bids: 17, Asks: 20.

This is the exact spread. The highest a buyer will pay right now is 7,554.25, and the lowest a seller will accept is 7,554.50.

  • The Green Box Below (7,551.25): This massive green bar (71 and 32) is a "Wall". It means an enormous amount of buying power is waiting to push the price back up if it drops to this level.

The Top Header

  • Last: 7,554.00 (The price of the most recent trade).
  • Change: -0.22% (Slightly negative for the day).
  • Purple "Ask": 7,554.50. The market is currently 0.50 ticks away from the last trade.

Part 2: How to Make Money From It (The Battle Strategy)

Randomly buying or selling loses money. You must interpret the "Story" of the order book.

1. The "Wall Bounce"

Trade: LIMIT BUY

What to look for: A massive green Bid wall (e.g., at 7,551.25 with 51.87% Imbalance).

Logic: Place a Limit Buy order exactly at the wall. When price drops to test it, the massive order absorbs the selling, causing a bounce upwards.

Crucial Rule: If the price punches through the wall, immediately cancel and reverse (Short sell).

2. The "Imbalance Fade"

Trade: LIMIT SELL

What to look for: Red Imbalance walls above the current price (e.g., at 7,554.50 with 54.05% Red, and 7,556.25 with 48.13% Red).

Logic: Place a Limit Sell order just below these ceilings. Bet against the temporary rise, knowing the massive red blocks will push the price back down.

3. Reacting to "HFT" Pings

Trade: MOMENTUM BUY

What to look for: Massive trades in the Time/Sales column (e.g., 100, 113, 17 contracts at 4:11:57) pushing the price up.

Logic: Watch the "Last" price. If a massive green buy comes in and pushes the price above the yellow line and holds for 5 seconds, Buy immediately. You are riding the coattails of the High-Frequency Traders breaking the level.

⚠️ The Golden Rules for Trading Level 2

  1. Don't trust the numbers 100%: At least 30% of the orders shown here are fake ("Iceberg" or "Spoofing"). Traders place massive walls to scare you, then pull them right before the price hits them.
  2. Volume is Truth, Depth is Lie: Watch the green/red bars, but only trade when the actual "Last" price matches the biggest bars. If the price drops but green bars grow, that is a bullish divergence.
  3. Protect your stop-loss: If you buy at a green wall and it drops one tick past that level, get out immediately. If the wall fails, the price usually drops 10-15 points in seconds.
