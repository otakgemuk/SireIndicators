Understanding every signal from SireIndicators

Institutional ORB + Brooks Box v3 Signals

Breakout Signals (BRK▲ / BRK▼)

These are the primary entry signals generated when price breaks above the Opening Range High (ORH) or below the Opening Range Low (ORL).

Signal Confluence Grade Meaning Action
BRK▲ A+ 7-8/8 (Excellent) Strong bullish breakout with maximum confluence Trade full size, BUY above signal bar close
BRK▲ A 5-6/8 (Good) Good bullish setup with solid confluence Trade standard size, reduce if trendless
BRK▲ B 3-4/8 (Marginal) Marginal bullish setup, multiple factors missing Consider reducing size or passing
BRK▲ C 0-2/8 (Poor) Poor bullish setup, low probability Avoid or use only micro-position
BRK▼ Varies Bearish breakout (mirror of bullish) Apply same logic in reverse
💡 Pro Tip: The confluence score considers:
  • ✓ Volume surge (above 1.2x average)
  • ✓ Volume spike (above 1.8x average)
  • ✓ VWAP bias alignment
  • ✓ 20 EMA position
  • ✓ Day type (Coil/Breakout/Trading Range)
  • ✓ Time of day quality
  • ✓ Gap status
  • ✓ ADR exhaustion

Failed Breakout Signals (FAIL▲ / FAIL▼)

These signals occur when price breaks beyond an OR level but then reverses back inside the OR within a specified number of bars.

Signal Setup Meaning Trading Strategy
FAIL▲ Failed breakdown attempt Price broke below ORL then reversed back up Bullish reversal - Buy above the broken level
FAIL▼ Failed breakdown attempt Price broke above ORH then reversed back down Bearish reversal - Sell below the broken level
📌 Strategy: Failed breakouts show weak directional commitment. Wait for price to move back toward the failed level to setup your entry. This creates a more favorable risk/reward.

Retest Signals (RT▲ / RT▼)

Retest signals occur when price returns to test an OR level after breaking it, showing potential continuation.

Signal Condition Meaning Trading Action
RT▲ Price above ORH, retest near ORL Support hold at broken level, bullish continuation Buy on close above retest high
RT▼ Price below ORL, retest near ORH Resistance hold at broken level, bearish continuation Sell on close below retest low

Confluence Score Grades

A+ Grade

Score: 7-8/8 (ORB) or 10-12/12 (R12R)

Excellent confluence. Multiple factors aligned. Trade at full size with confidence.

A Grade

Score: 5-6/8 (ORB) or 8-9/12 (R12R)

Good confluence. Most factors aligned. Trade standard size.

B Grade

Score: 3-4/8 (ORB) or 6-7/12 (R12R)

Moderate confluence. Some factors missing. Reduce size or pass.

C Grade

Score: 0-2/8 (ORB) or <6/12 (R12R)

Poor confluence. Multiple factors missing. Avoid or use micro-position only.

Key Metrics Explained

📊 Confluence Score (ORB: 0-8 | R12R: 0-12)

A composite score considering all active filters and technical factors. Higher score = higher probability setup.

📈 ADR (Average Daily Range)

8-day average of the daily range. Used to assess:

  • ADR Consumption: How much of today's expected range has been used
  • Breakout Probability: Lower ADR % = more room to run
  • Day Classification: Coil (<30%), Breakout (30-50%), Trading Range (>50%)

💨 Volume Context

Surge Volume > 1.2x average - adequate volume for breakout
Spike Volume > 1.8x average - high conviction move
Low Volume < 0.5x average - weak move, suspect signals

⏰ Time-of-Day Context

PRIME (10:30-11:30 ET, 14:00-15:00 ET) High probability trading windows - full trade size
NEUTRAL (other times) Average probability - standard size
CAUTION (9:00-10:00 ET, 11:30-13:30 ET) Low probability windows - reduce size or avoid
